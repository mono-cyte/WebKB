# 天体轨迹方程
## 定理

1. 能量守恒
2. 角动量守恒 $\vec{L} = \vec{r} \times \vec{p}$

## 推导

以 $\infty$ 为 0 势能点, 由万有引力公式 $F = G \frac{Mm}{r^2}$, 得:
1. 重力势能 $E_p = -G \frac{Mm}{r_0}$
2. 动能 $E_k = \frac{1}{2}mv^2$

由能量守恒, 得:
$E_p + E_k = E$, 其中 $E$ 为常量

分解速度
$\vec{v}_{\theta} = \vec{\omega} \times \vec{r}$

> $\vec{\omega} = \frac{\vec{r} \times {\vec{v_{\theta}}}}{|\vec{r}|^2}$
> 
> $\omega = \frac{\mathrm{d}\theta}{\mathrm{d}t} = \frac{v}{r} = \frac{L}{mr^2}$
> 
> $v_{\theta} = \omega r = \frac{\mathrm{d}\theta}{\mathrm{d}t} r = \frac{L}{mr}$

$\vec{v_{r}} = \frac{\mathrm{d}\vec{r}}{\mathrm{d}t}$

> $v_{r} = \frac{\mathrm{d}r}{\mathrm{d}t}$


$E = E_k + E_p = \frac{1}{2}mv_{r}^2 + \frac{1}{2}mv_{\theta}^2 -G \frac{Mm}{r}$

直接找 $\theta$, $r$ 关系难以分离, 转而寻找微分关系

$E = \frac{1}{2}m(\frac{\mathrm{d}r}{\mathrm{d}t})^2 + \frac{L^2}{2mr^2}-G \frac{Mm}{r}$

$$
\begin{cases}
\frac{\mathrm{d}r}{\mathrm{d}t} = \sqrt{\frac{2E}{m} - \frac{L^2}{m^2r^2} + 2G \frac{M}{r}}\\
\frac{\mathrm{d}\theta}{\mathrm{d}t} = \frac{L}{mr^2}
\end{cases}
$$
$\frac{\mathrm{d}\theta}{\mathrm{d}r} = \frac{\frac{L}{mr^2}}{\sqrt{\frac{2E}{m} - \frac{L^2}{m^2r^2} + 2G \frac{M}{r}}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{\frac{2Em}{L^2}-\frac{1}{r^2}+\frac{2GMm^2}{L^2r}}} \cdot \frac{1}{r^2}$
$\mathrm{d}\theta = \frac{1}{\sqrt{\frac{2Em}{L^2}-\frac{1}{r^2}+\frac{2GMm^2}{L^2r}}} \cdot \frac{1}{r^2} \mathrm{d}r = \frac{1}{\sqrt{\frac{2Em}{L^2}-\frac{1}{r^2}+\frac{2GMm^2}{L^2r}}} \cdot \mathrm{d}(-\frac{1}{r}) = \frac{-1}{\sqrt{-(\frac{1}{r}-\frac{GMm^2}{L^2})^2 + \frac{G^2M^2m^4}{L^4}+\frac{2Em}{L^2}}}  \cdot \mathrm{d}(\frac{1}{r})$

令 $A=\frac{GMm^2}{L^2}$, $B=\frac{G^2M^2m^4}{L^4}+\frac{2Em}{L^2}$, $u=\frac{1}{r}$

$\mathrm{d}\theta = \frac{-1}{\sqrt{-(u-A)^2+B}} \cdot \mathrm{d}u$

令 $v = \frac{u-A}{\sqrt{B}}$, $\mathrm{d}v = \frac{\mathrm{d}u}{\sqrt{B}}$

$\mathrm{d}\theta = \frac{-1}{\sqrt{-B(v)^2 + B}} \cdot \sqrt{B} \mathrm{d}v = -\frac{1}{\sqrt{1-(v)^2}} \cdot \mathrm{d}v$ 

令 $\sin{\alpha} = v$

$\int \mathrm{d}\theta = \int -\frac{1}{\sqrt{1-(v)^2}} \cdot \mathrm{d}v = \int -\frac{1}{\sqrt{1-\sin^2{\alpha}}} \cdot \mathrm{d}\sin{\alpha} = \int -\frac{\cos \alpha}{\cos \alpha} \cdot \mathrm{d}\alpha = -\int \mathrm{d}\alpha$

$\theta = \alpha + C = \sin^{-1} v + C$
$\sin(\theta + C) = v = \frac{u-A}{\sqrt{B}} = \frac{\frac{1}{r}-A}{\sqrt{B}}$
因为 $C$ 仅决定周期的相位, 可忽略: $C = 0$
$r=\frac{1}{\sqrt{B} \sin\theta + A} = \frac{\frac{1}{A}}{1 + \frac{\sqrt{B}}{A} \sin\theta} = \frac{\frac{L^2}{GMm^2}}{1+\sqrt{1+\frac{2EL^2}{G^2M^2m^3}}\sin\theta}$
根据[圆锥曲线](圆锥曲线.md)方程形式可知:
此方程可视为 离心率 $e =  \frac{\sqrt{B}}{A} = \sqrt{1+\frac{2EL^2}{G^2M^2m^3}}$ 的圆锥曲线
