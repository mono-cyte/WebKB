# 运算法则



> [!NOTION]
> 编程中，将多维数组元素优先作为**列向量**，以契合下列运算


## 加 Addition

$$
\begin{bmatrix}
a_{11} & \cdots & a_{1m}\\
\vdots & \ddots & \vdots\\
a_{n1} & \cdots & a_{nm}\\
\end{bmatrix}_{n \times m}
+
\begin{bmatrix}
b_{11} & \cdots & b_{1m}\\
\vdots & \ddots & \vdots\\
b_{n1} & \cdots & b_{nm}\\
\end{bmatrix}_{n \times m}
=
\begin{bmatrix}
a_{11} + b_{11} & \cdots & a_{1m} + b_{1m}\\
\vdots & \ddots & \vdots\\
a_{n1} + b_{n1} & \cdots & a_{nm} + b_{nm}\\
\end{bmatrix}_{n \times m}
$$

## 减 Subtraction

$$
\begin{bmatrix}
a_{11} & \cdots & a_{1m}\\
\vdots & \ddots & \vdots\\
a_{n1} & \cdots & a_{nm}\\
\end{bmatrix}_{n \times m}
-
\begin{bmatrix}
b_{11} & \cdots & b_{1m}\\
\vdots & \ddots & \vdots\\
b_{n1} & \cdots & b_{nm}\\
\end{bmatrix}_{n \times m}
=
\begin{bmatrix}
a_{11} - b_{11} & \cdots & a_{1m} - b_{1m}\\
\vdots & \ddots & \vdots\\
a_{n1} - b_{n1} & \cdots & a_{nm} - b_{nm}\\
\end{bmatrix}_{n \times m}
$$
## 数乘

$$
k \times
\begin{bmatrix}
a_{11} & \cdots & a_{1m}\\
\vdots & \ddots & \vdots\\
a_{n1} & \cdots & a_{nm}\\
\end{bmatrix}_{n \times m}
=
\begin{bmatrix}
k \times a_{11} & \cdots & k \times a_{1m}\\
\vdots & \ddots & \vdots\\
k \times a_{n1} & \cdots & k \times a_{nm}\\
\end{bmatrix}_{n \times m}
$$
