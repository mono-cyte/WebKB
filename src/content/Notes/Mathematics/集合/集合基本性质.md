# 集合基本性质

$$
S \cup \emptyset = S - \emptyset = S
$$
$$
S \cap \emptyset = \emptyset
$$
$$
\overline{\emptyset} = U
$$
$$
\overline{\overline{S}} = S
$$
$$
S - T  = S \cap \overline{T}
$$
$$
\text{if } S_{1} \subseteq S_{2} \text{ then } \overline{S_{2}} \subseteq \overline{S_{1}}
$$
$$
|2^{S}| = 2^{|S|}
$$
$$
|S_{1} \cup S_{2}| \leq |S_{1}| + |S_{2}|
$$
$$
| S_{1} \times S_{2} | = |S_{1}||S_{2}| 
$$
