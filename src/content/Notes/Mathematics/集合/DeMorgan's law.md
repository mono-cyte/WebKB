# DeMorgan's law

$$
\overline{S_{1} \cup S_{1}} = \overline{S_{1}} \cap \overline{S_{1}}
$$
或
$$
\overline{\bigcup_{p \in P} S_{p}} = \bigcap_{p \in P} \overline{S_{p}}
$$
---
$$
\overline{S_{1} \cap S_{1}} = \overline{S_{1}} \cup \overline{S_{1}}
$$
或
$$
\overline{\bigcap_{p \in P} S_{p}} = \bigcup_{p \in P} \overline{S_{p}}
$$
